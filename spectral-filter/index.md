# Spectral and Frequency-Domain Analysis

Second-order structure by frequency instead of by lag: correlograms, periodograms, smoothed and parametric spectral density, cross-spectra, and theoretical filter transfer functions.

* [Correlograms and Spectral Density](spectral-density.md) - Sample ACF/PACF/CCF with Ljung-Box diagnostics, the raw periodogram, and Welch/smoothed/AR spectral density estimates.
* [Cross-Spectra and Transfer Functions](spectral-cross.md) - Two-series coherence/phase/gain and the theoretical frequency response of HP, BK, Hamilton, and ideal filters (no data required).
