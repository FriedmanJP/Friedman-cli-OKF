# DSGE

Representative-agent solution and analysis, full Bayesian estimation workflow, parameterized heterogeneous-agent family models (Bewley banks, continuous-time, DCEGM, Khan-Thomas firms, life-cycle OLG, Blanchard OLG), and one-household HA-DSGE (SSJ/Reiter/Krusell-Smith) — the 51 `friedman dsge` plus 11 `friedman hadsge` leaves.

* [RA Core: Solve and Analyze](core.md) - Steady state, 7-method solve, determinacy maps, simulation, IRF/FEVD/HD, moments, perfect foresight, GMM estimation (10 leaves).
* [Bayesian DSGE](bayes.md) - The `dsge bayes` node: estimation, posterior analysis, diagnostics, and predictive checks (15 leaves).
* [Structured Family Models](family.md) - `bank`, `ct`, `dcegm`, `firm`, `lifecycle`, `olg` sub-nodes with builtin parameters (26 leaves).
* [HA-DSGE](hadsge.md) - `hadsge` steady state, SSJ/Reiter/KS solve, aggregate and distributional IRFs, panel simulation, accuracy, Bayes (11 leaves).
